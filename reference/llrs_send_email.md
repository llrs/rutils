# Send a quick email

Send a quick email

## Usage

``` r
llrs_send_email(text, subject, to, attachments = NULL)
```

## Arguments

- text:

  Text of the email?

- subject:

  What is the subject of the email?

- to:

  Address to send the emails (no CC or BCC)

- attachments:

  Paths to files.
